# Paper illustrations

`problem.png` and `framework.png` are raster renderings of the problem and
framework figures supplied for **IMTS-Tokenizer: Time-Aware Tokenization for
Irregular Multivariate Time Series Forecasting**, NeurIPS 2026.

Authors: Bin Xu, Yinghua Li, Linqi Han, Xiaoyu Li, Xinghao Yang, Wei Liu and
Yongshun Gong. Bin Xu and Yinghua Li contributed equally.
